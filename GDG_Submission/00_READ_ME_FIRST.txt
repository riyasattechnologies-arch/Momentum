DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------------------------------
Momentum is an AI chief of staff for your whole life: career, health,
money and learning. It interviews you about your goals and fixed
commitments, proposes a plan you negotiate and approve, builds each day
around your real schedule with P1/P2/P3 tasks, reminds you before every
block, and re-plans when life happens. The AI proposes every change;
you approve it.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Google Gemini and a tested deterministic
scheduler (11 automated tests). Deployed on Vercel.
